# Suggested content to write

Ordered by how often a visitor is likely to hit it.

## Stories — target 15 to 25

Cover at least one per theme so retrieval has something for each:

- [ ] A call you got wrong, and what you changed after
- [ ] Disagreeing with a CEO or board, and how it landed
- [ ] Cutting scope under a hard deadline
- [ ] Firing or performance-managing someone
- [ ] Hiring someone who turned out great — what you saw
- [ ] Hiring someone who didn't work out — what you missed
- [ ] The .NET 4 to .NET 8 migration at iScribeHealth (70% downtime cut — what it actually took)
- [ ] Getting through the first pen test at iScribeHealth
- [ ] Raising the $4M for Heali — what nearly killed the round
- [ ] Winding down or pivoting Heali
- [ ] The 100fps logo detection at A9 — the constraint that made it hard
- [ ] Running 40+ concurrent projects at Snap without it collapsing
- [ ] A technical bet that paid off
- [ ] A technical bet that didn't
- [ ] Something you built that nobody used

## Perspective — target 8 to 12

- [ ] How you think about technical debt
- [ ] When to build vs. buy vs. wait
- [ ] What you look for when hiring engineers
- [ ] How you evaluate an AI product idea
- [ ] Research vs. product — how you decide what ships
- [ ] Managing engineers vs. managing managers
- [ ] How you approach compliance work without killing velocity
- [ ] What you learned going from big tech to founding

## Logistics — the questions you'll actually get

- [ ] What kind of role you're looking for now
- [ ] Location and remote preferences
- [ ] Company stage and size you work best at
- [ ] What you're not interested in
- [ ] Availability and notice
