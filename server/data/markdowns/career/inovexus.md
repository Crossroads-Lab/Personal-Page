# Inovexus

## What are you doing now besides Crossroads?

I've been a Mentor and Advisor at Inovexus since July 2023, alongside running Crossroads Venture Studio.

## What does the mentoring involve?

Giving back to entrepreneurs by sharing what I've learned on both the technical and the product-executive side — which are usually the two things a technical founder is trying to hold at once, badly, the way I was.

I'm currently mentoring two startups, invyo.io and quideos.com. One of them raised $2M in seed funding after nine months in the programme.

## Why do you spend time on mentoring?

Because the gap I'm helping with is the one that nearly cost me at Heali. I had the engineering. What I didn't have was the operational and commercial instinct, and nobody was sitting next to me to shorten that learning curve.

It's the same reason I started Crossroads Venture Studio in 2023. Mentoring is the lighter-weight version of the same instinct: technical founders don't usually need someone to check their architecture, they need someone who has already made the expensive mistakes to tell them which ones are coming.
