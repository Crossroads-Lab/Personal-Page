# Snap Inc.

## Role and dates

I was a Technical Program Manager and Senior Research Scientist at Snap from May 2016 to May 2019.

## Scope

I directed a 50+ person R&D organization across 40+ concurrent projects, shipping research into global products serving 188M+ daily active users. I expanded the group into 10+ research domains including face analysis, NLP, scene understanding, augmented reality, and video analysis.

## Research that shipped

Privacy-preserving friend recommendations revived 14% of inactive users and increased daily engagement by 7%. Bitmoji chat recommendations drove an 8% engagement increase through automated interface triggers. Sentiment-based ticket sorting used NLP to increase support productivity by 71% in ticket resolution volume.

## Cross-functional work

I coordinated stakeholder management across five departments — business, design, data science, engineering, and legal — to align R&D roadmaps with global product strategy.

## M&A and IP

I was a core member of the Technical Acquisition Committee, evaluating external technologies for acquisition. I authored 8 patents and 7 publications during this period.
