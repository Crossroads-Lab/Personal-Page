# iScribeHealth

## What was your role at iScribeHealth and when?

I was VP of Engineering at iScribeHealth from January 2025 to July 2025, running a 10-person engineering organization on a $2M annual budget.

## What did you own at iScribeHealth? What were you responsible for?

I owned engineering delivery, platform reliability, and production readiness across every customer-facing system. I partnered with the CEO and product stakeholders to align the technical roadmap and OKRs against a $100M ARR growth target for AI-driven clinical documentation.

## What platform and reliability work did you do at iScribeHealth?

I led the platform migration from .NET 4 to .NET 8, which cut production downtime by 70%. I also managed the authentication migration from Azure Entra ID to Auth0.

## How did you get iScribeHealth to SOC 2 and HIPAA?

I guided the organization to SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance, and oversaw remediation of 100% of the high-severity vulnerabilities surfaced by the company's first-ever penetration test.

## What AI products did you ship at iScribeHealth?

I drove delivery of AI-driven E&M coding suggestions, which improved provider billing outcomes by 10 to 20%. I also expanded our integration with Athena's ambient scribe system, covering 200+ customers.

## Who did you hire at iScribeHealth? How did you build the team?

I recruited a Lead Engineer and a Product Manager to strengthen cross-functional execution, and drove alignment across product, engineering, and customer success.
