# Google and YouTube

## Role and dates

I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Google on YouTube from July 2011 to November 2012.

## Suggested Edits

I initiated and supervised the "Suggested Edits" project, a self-correcting system for large-scale video quality detection and correction. I engineered and scaled the feature across the entire YouTube video corpus, impacting 94M+ daily active users and improving global video playback quality.

## Creator tools

I created 10+ distinct video effects and filters, integrating complex computer vision algorithms into user-facing creator tools. I secured 2 patents for novel video processing and enhancement techniques.
