# Amazon A9

## What was your role at Amazon and when?

I was a Machine Learning Researcher and Technical Product Manager at Amazon's A9 visual search team from November 2012 to May 2016, building high-scale computer vision products serving 200M+ daily active users across the global Amazon ecosystem.

## What did you build at Amazon A9?

I launched the mobile brand logo detection and recognition project, achieving real-time performance at 100 frames per second on mobile hardware.

## What infrastructure did you build at Amazon?

I blueprinted the global visual search database and the proprietary annotation and evaluation platform, managing a corpus of 12M+ images and labels.

## How many patents do you have from Amazon?

I authored and was granted 9 patents for innovations in visual search, object recognition, and machine learning infrastructure. I guided the full product lifecycle from R&D through production, making sure technical feasibility matched consumer requirements.
