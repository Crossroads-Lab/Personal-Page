# Heali AI

## Role and dates

I co-founded Heali AI and served as Chief Science Officer and Head of Product from May 2019 to March 2023. It was an AI-driven HealthTech startup focused on personalized nutrition for chronic disease management.

## What I did

I shaped the product vision and translated complex nutritional science into a scalable B2B and B2C platform. I ran discovery, established the prioritization framework, and launched consumer applications on iOS and Android.

## Fundraising and outcome

I secured $4M in seed funding and reached an 8-digit business valuation by establishing the company's core intellectual property and technical roadmap.

## The AI and data engineering

I architected a semi-supervised AI pipeline that aggregated and cleaned more than 2 billion nutrition data points, producing a proprietary engine that supported 200+ medical conditions.

## Growth and partnerships

The consumer apps scaled to 10,000+ active users. I bootstrapped a CDC-compliant Diabetes Prevention Program product and secured two nationwide healthcare partnerships.

## Team and IP

I recruited and directed a multidisciplinary organization of up to 12 engineers, product designers, and UX researchers. I authored one patent and one peer-reviewed publication tied directly to the platform's core AI innovations.
