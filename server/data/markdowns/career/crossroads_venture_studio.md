# Crossroads Venture Studio

## What is your current role? What are you doing now?

I'm Founder and General Partner at Crossroads Venture Studio, from March 2023 to the present. I direct technical architecture and product-market-fit strategy across a portfolio of AI-driven ventures.

## What does Crossroads Venture Studio do?

The studio incubates AI ventures. I run technical validation and go-to-market execution for the teams that come through it — 20+ internal and external teams so far. For portfolio companies I serve as the primary technical architect, defining AI orchestration and infrastructure strategy.

## What results has the studio produced?

61.5% of portfolio ventures have reached seed funding or first-customer bootstrapping. I built a proprietary incubation framework to accelerate AI product delivery, which cut time-to-market for generative AI features and standardized CI/CD pipelines across the portfolio.
