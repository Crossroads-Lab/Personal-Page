# Infineon Technologies

## What was your role at Infineon and when?

I was a QA Application Engineer at Infineon Technologies in Munich, Germany, from July 2003 to July 2004. This was early-career systems work, and it was the year I spent abroad after missing the cut for a place in the US on my first attempt. I didn't speak a word of German when I arrived.

## What did you build at Infineon?

I engineered test platforms and optimized hardware drivers in C and Assembly, reducing validation cycle time by 30%. I also built TCL scripts for electronic board test automation, cutting manual testing effort by 40%.
