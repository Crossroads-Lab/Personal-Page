# What I'd do in the first 90 days

## What are the first things you would do if you got hired?

First, assess the current architecture, the team's strengths, and the practices already in place. Second, identify the top engineering bottlenecks and fix them, while aligning with leadership on priorities. Third, ship a quick win to build momentum and trust.

## Why would you do those things in that order?

The assessment has to come first or you're solving the problems you brought with you rather than the ones that are there. The quick win comes last but matters more than it sounds — a new engineering leader has no credit, and shipping something real is how you get the room to do the bigger work.
