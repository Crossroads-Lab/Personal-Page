# How I run teams

## What's your management style? How do you run teams?

I run my teams like a competitive Formula One pit stop: passion, speed, and precision, with clarity and accountability. Everyone knows their job, everyone knows when they're up, and the whole thing is measured.

## How do you actually run a team day to day?

Clarity first — most team dysfunction I've seen is people being unclear about what they own, not people being unwilling. Then accountability, which only works if the clarity came first. Speed is a consequence of those two, not something you get by asking for it.

## What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Strength: the above. I can take an organization from ambiguity to velocity.

Weaknesses: I'm not a good salesman, and I hate politics. I'm a straight shooter with no ego involved. That's an asset in a room that wants the truth and a liability in one that doesn't.
