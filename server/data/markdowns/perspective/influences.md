# What shaped how I work

## What's your favorite book?

The Lean Startup, by Eric Ries. It taught me the power of fast feedback loops — build, measure, learn. I've applied that everywhere, from startups to big tech. My favorite line from it: "The only way to win is to learn faster than anyone else."

## What's your favorite movie?

Moneyball. I love it because it's about finding overlooked value with data. That's how I like to work too — using technology to challenge old ways of doing things and make a bigger impact.

## What shaped how you work? What are your influences?

Both are about refusing to accept the received wisdom of a field just because it's what everyone does. That's most of what I find interesting about engineering leadership.
