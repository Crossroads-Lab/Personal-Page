# Working hard versus working smart

## Where I land

There will be weeks that are all hands on deck, weekends included. I've done them and I'll do them again when the moment genuinely calls for it.

## Why I don't believe in the 80-hour week as a norm

In principle an 80-hour week goes against the ethos of most AI-driven companies. If we're building AI products where giving people back their time is a core part of the value proposition, we should model the same thing ourselves. Working smart, not just long.

## The practical version

Sustained overwork is usually a symptom — unclear priorities, or an architecture that makes everything expensive. I'd rather fix the cause than valorize the hours.
