# Where healthcare AI goes next

## What will the industry look like in five years?

AI agents will handle the majority of administrative and clinical workflows. No archaic EHR middleman. Healthcare gets faster, cheaper, more precise, and accessible to more people.

## What would you like to build if you joined us?

Robust AI agents for healthcare — systems that connect patients, providers, and health insurance directly. The version I want: you go to your doctor, and by the time you leave you're already reimbursed, because the level of trust in the technology is such that reimbursement approval can be instant.

## Why is that the right problem to work on?

It's the workflow where the administrative burden is most obviously indefensible, and where getting it right compounds. Every hour a clinician doesn't spend on paperwork is an hour of actual care.
