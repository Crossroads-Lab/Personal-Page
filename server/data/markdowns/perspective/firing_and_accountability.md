# How I think about firing people

## How do you feel about firing people?

It's one of the hardest parts of leadership, and sometimes it's necessary. I do it with objectivity, compassion, respect, and accountability.

## How do you think about accountability when someone underperforms?

A failed employee often comes with failed leadership. Before I conclude someone can't do the job, I want to know whether they were given a clear definition of it, honest feedback early enough to act on, and the support the role actually required. If any of those are missing, that's my failure first.

## Why does leadership matter when someone fails?

It changes what you do. If the answer is that the person was set up badly, the fix is upstream and firing them just moves the problem to the next hire.
