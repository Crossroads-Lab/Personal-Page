# What I ask in interviews

## Do you have any questions for me?

What's the biggest engineering challenge your company is facing today? And how do you see me driving your mission forward in the next twelve months?

## Why do you ask those questions?

The first tells me whether the hard problem is one I'd be good at, and whether people are honest about it. The second tells me whether they've actually thought about what they want from this role, or are hiring a title.
