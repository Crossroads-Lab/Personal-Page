# Leaving iScribeHealth

## What iScribeHealth does

iScribeHealth builds AI-powered scribing, coding, and pre-authorization tools that save clinicians time and reduce administrative burden.

## Why I moved on

I'm proud of what I built there. I was looking for a mission-driven role where I could scale engineering faster and have a bigger impact on saving lives. Nothing went wrong — I'd done the re-architecture, the compliance work, and the integrations, and the next chapter I wanted wasn't the one in front of me.
