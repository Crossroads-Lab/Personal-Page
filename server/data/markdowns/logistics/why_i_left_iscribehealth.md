# Leaving iScribeHealth

## What does iScribeHealth do?

iScribeHealth builds AI-powered scribing, coding, and pre-authorization tools that save clinicians time and reduce administrative burden.

## Why did you leave your last role? What happened at iScribeHealth?

I'm proud of what I built there — the re-architecture, the compliance work, and the integrations, with production downtime down 70%. The role ended while I was part-way through reorganising the engineering team.

What I'm looking for next is a mission-driven role where I can scale engineering faster and have a bigger impact on saving lives. I'd rather talk that through directly than write it up here.
