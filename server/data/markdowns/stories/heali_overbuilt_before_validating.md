# Overbuilding before validating at Heali

## The mistake

Early at Heali we overbuilt features before validating demand. We had a strong technical thesis and we kept building against it instead of putting something small in front of users and finding out.

## What it cost

Time and runway, on a startup where both were the scarce resource. Work that was genuinely good got built for a demand curve we hadn't confirmed existed.

## What I changed

I learned to prioritize fast iteration and user feedback before scaling a solution. Build the smallest thing that produces a real signal, then let the signal tell you what to build next. It's the discipline I now apply by default, and it's why I'm skeptical of roadmaps that go more than a quarter out without a feedback checkpoint.
