# Building the early team at Heali

## The team

At Heali, the health-tech startup I co-founded, I recruited and led a multidisciplinary team of up to 12 — engineers, product designers, and UX researchers. At that size every hire still visibly changes the culture and there's nowhere to hide a bad one.

## What we did with it

We architected a robust and precise AI pipeline, launched a fast-growing B2C app that reached 10K+ users, and helped raise $4M in funding.

## The values that emerged

Speed and customer satisfaction. We didn't set those at the start and post them on a wall — they're what the team actually optimized for once it was under real pressure, and naming them afterward is what made them stick.
