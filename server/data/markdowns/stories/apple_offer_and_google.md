# The Apple offer that vanished

## How did you end up at Google? Was that the plan?

It wasn't. I wanted Apple.

That came from the same place as the graphics ambition — I came at this with a creative and artistic streak, and what I really wanted was to work in CGI. Apple was the company that fitted that version of me.

I interviewed there and I passed. Then they retracted the offer: hiring freeze.

## What happened after the offer was pulled?

This is the part I still find absurd. The same day the bad news arrived, Google sent me their offer. A few hours apart.

I went to Google on YouTube instead, and that became the next phase of my career rather than the one I'd been picturing.

## Do you regret not getting the Apple job?

Not now, though I wouldn't have said that at the time.

There's a joke in how it turned out. I wanted the creative, visual work, I lost the job that represented it, and then at YouTube I spent my time building video effects and filters — a dozen or so of them — putting computer vision into tools creators actually used. I got the work. I just didn't get it from the company I'd auditioned for.

Between this and ending up in AI after being rejected from AI, I've stopped treating a closed door as information about where I'm going.
