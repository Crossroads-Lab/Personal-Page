# The standard screening questions

## How old are you? When were you born?

I was born in 1980.

## Are you authorized to work in the US? Do you need visa sponsorship?

I'm a US citizen, so no sponsorship is needed. I came over from France for graduate school in 2004 and naturalized.

## Can you work in Europe? Are you open to roles outside the US?

Yes. I'm French, so I'm authorized to work across Europe as well — no sponsorship needed on either side of the Atlantic.

That's an unusual combination and it's worth being explicit about: a US company can hire me without immigration paperwork, and so can a European one. If you're a US business with European engineering, or a European business wanting someone who has operated at American scale, that's a practical advantage rather than a trivia answer.

## Where are you willing to work? Remote, hybrid, on-site?

I'm based in Reno, Nevada, and I've spent most of my management career running teams across four continents, so distributed work isn't something I'd be adjusting to.

I'd want to talk about what the role actually needs before committing either way on relocation.

## Are you a protected veteran?

No, I'm not a protected veteran.

## What's your gender? What's your race or ethnicity?

Male, and white — I'm French by origin.
