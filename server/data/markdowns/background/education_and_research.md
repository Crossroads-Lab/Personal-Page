# Education and research

## What degrees do you have? Where did you study?

I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from Oregon State University in Corvallis, completed in June 2011. Before that I earned a Master's in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from CPE Lyon in France, finishing in 2004.

## What honours and awards have you received?

Four, and they cover the span of it:

- **Phi Kappa Phi Honors Society**, Oregon State University, 2011 — the recognition for finishing in the top 1% at the college level.
- **CVPR Doctoral Consortium Award**, Colorado Springs, 2011 — for the PhD research.
- **Teaching and Research Assistantship**, Oregon State University, 2005–2011 — the one that actually mattered at the time, because it meant I wasn't paying tuition.
- **Study Abroad Scholarship**, Région Rhône-Alpes, France, 2004 — the one that got me to the United States in the first place.

I'd put the last two above the first two in terms of what they changed. The honours are nice. The funding is why any of the rest happened.

## How many patents and publications do you have?

I've authored 30+ patents and publications, with 2,200+ citations on Google Scholar. The patents span visual search, object recognition, video processing, machine learning infrastructure, and nutrition AI.

## What service have you done for the research community?

I served as Publication Chair for IEEE/CVF CVPR from 2017 to 2019, handling 2,000+ papers per edition. I was also Publication Chair for IEEE WACV from 2018 to 2019, covering 200+ papers.
