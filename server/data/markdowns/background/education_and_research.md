# Education and research

I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from Oregon State University in Corvallis, completed in June 2011. Before that I earned a Master's in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from CPE Lyon in France, in June 2005.

I've authored 30+ patents and publications, with 2,200+ citations on Google Scholar. The patents span visual search, object recognition, video processing, machine learning infrastructure, and nutrition AI.
